## Goal
On the local demo stack, an invited (synthetic) vendor signs in, must accept the Vendor Pilot Agreement before anything opens, and then sees a read-only dashboard (ownership mix, purchase and wishlist trends, condition spectrum, one filter at a time), gaps ranked by gap score, reviewed reports and its account page with the privacy contact address, and can upload, check, fix, publish, update and withdraw a product feed whose items then appear in the consumer catalogue marked with the vendor's name; every figure comes from a nightly aggregation of eligible participants' attributes only, with no segment under 10 users, counts rounded to 5, complementary suppression, no Wardrobe unlock wishlist entry and no image, receipt, note or free text; every answered vendor query is logged and a vendor is refused after 200 calls in an hour; an admin sees the aggregation runs and the query log, generates a report and marks it reviewed.

## Glossary
- "Counted lines": changed lines per `git diff --numstat` against the base, excluding package-lock.json, JSON data files, evidence and screenshots. Tests count. A maker whose estimate passes 650 before starting tells the orchestrator, who splits the work order; one that passes 650 while building says so at once (R6a retro).
- "Eligible user": `app.profiles.role = 'consumer'`, `deleting = false` and `ops.is_eligible(id)` (LLD 5.1 step 3; maintenance does not affect it, LLD 9.1).
- "Synthetic": produced by code in this repository from a fixed seed, invented names, `@example.test` addresses; never copied from a real person, store or brand.
- "Price bands" (LLD 5.1, amounts in USD): codes `<25`, `25-50`, `50-100`, `100-200`, `200+`, `Unknown`; lower bound inclusive. USD needs no rate; another currency converts as `price / per_usd` of its latest `ops.fx_rates` row.
- "Region map" (FR-85; LLD gives none; LOI-4 is open): `lk_region` = `South Asia`; `country_region` = `IN -> South Asia`; any other country `Unknown`. The smallest map that serves the only supported country; the owner may change it (O3).
- "Letter sizes" (LLD 4.2 `lk_size`, no list in the documents): `Letter` system only, codes `XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `XXL`, `XXXL`; any other size maps to `Other`. `lk_brand` stays empty in migrations and seeds (a real brand list is the owner's, O3); the demo seed inserts only invented brands, so typed brands outside it count as `Other`.
- "Dimensions" (the whitelist of LLD 5.2): `category`, `colour_family`, `fit`, `brand`, `category_colour_family`, `category_fit`, `category_brand`; "filters": `size`, `season`, `price_band`, `region`. Any other name raises `invalid_dimension`.
- "Items included" (FR-86): the number of `owned` fact rows of the run; "users included": distinct `user_hash` over all facts; "rows suppressed": cube rows deleted by LLD 5.3 steps 1, 2 and 4.
- "Share" in ownership mix (FR-72 says share; LLD 5.4 returns counts): segment `users_rounded` / coverage `users_rounded`, shown as a whole percent next to the rounded count; both inputs are published values, so it reveals nothing new.
- "Rising wishlist segment" (FR-78, undefined): a `category` whose `wishlist` `users_rounded` in the report month is higher than in the month before, both rows visible; ranked by the increase, top 10.
- "Shared colour-name table": `packages/taxonomy/src/colour-names.json`: the 17 swatch family names and the LLD 8.1 step 5 synonyms (navy, khaki, beige, olive, maroon, burgundy, charcoal, tan, camel), each mapped to its family's reference swatch hex; no CSS-name list (smallest set; a name outside it is refused with `unknown_colour`).
- "Vendor events": recorded through the existing browser-local `EventLog` (lib/events.ts) like every other event in Release 0, with no properties.
- "Fixed error codes" of this release: `forbidden`, `agreement_required`, `maintenance`, `rate_limited`, `invalid_dimension`, `unsupported_period`, `invalid_top_n`, `aggregation_running`, `invalid_min_group_size`, `already_reviewed`, `upload_not_found`, plus the per-row feed codes listed in WO-0108's notes file.
