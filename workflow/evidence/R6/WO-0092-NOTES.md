## Finding for the checker and WO-0081 (not fixed here, outside the work order)
The update policy `receipts_update` requires `job_id is null or the job exists as the caller's receipt job`. The daily purge deletes receipt jobs 7 days after they finish (and receipts.job_id has no key into jobs, by design), so after the purge EVERY update of a confirmed receipt by its owner (for example correcting the store name) fails with 42501, even one that leaves job_id as it is. My same-value test therefore uses receipt 14 (its job still exists). A fix would move the job-ownership check from the policy to the write-once trigger (check only when job_id changes from null) and drop it from the policy; that changes M26 of WO-0080 and needs a decision, so I left it. Until then the app must not edit confirmed receipts through the user's session after 7 days.
