5. Nav and layout:
   - Nav at 375px: one clean row, no orphan, with all five links (WARDROBE OUTFITS WISHLIST PURCHASES SETTINGS) at full length. Row 1 is wordmark, DEMO label and SIGN OUT. Because labels were not shortened, no aria-label was needed (visible text equals the accessible name; the existing tests pin the visible names `Wardrobe`, `Outfits`, `Settings` too).

The base `button.linklike` rule keeps `color: var(--link)` because components/auth/a11y.test.ts reads the FIRST `button.linklike` rule and requires `color: var(--link)`, `background: none` and `min-height: 44px`; that test is untouched and passes, and the black override is a later, more specific rule (both colours are covered by the contrast pairs).
   - Wordmark 18px (token `--fs-brand`, computed 18px).
   - Archive rows align across tile columns: `.item` stretches to the row, the grid has rows `auto auto 1fr auto`, so the meta row absorbs the slack and the Archive or Restore row sits at the bottom; plus `.item .muted { min-height: 40px }`. Browser: summary tops 773/773, 1120/1120, 1468/1468 at 375px.
   - `input[aria-invalid='true'], select[aria-invalid='true']` 2px border (padding reduced by 1px so nothing moves). Computed 2px in the browser. The selector carries the element name because `input[type='email']` has higher specificity than a bare attribute selector (found in the browser, the first version computed 1px).

At 1024px and up, `article` (the legal pages, rendered by components/consent/LegalPage) is a two-column grid: the sticky heading on the left, the text column on the right (desktop-08).
