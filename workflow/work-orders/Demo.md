## Goal
Every screen of the app looks like one coherent product in the visual language below, still accessible (contrast tests, keyboard, reduced motion), and there is a one-command demo pack: demo accounts with realistic synthetic wardrobes, a flagged synthetic demo catalogue and a written demo script a stakeholder walk-through can follow.

## Design direction (measured from the reference site on 2026-11-07; take the STYLE, never the brand)
- Ground white `#FFFFFF`, text black `#000000`; one electric-blue accent (about `#1B2BFF`, not an exact copy of the reference) for links, active states and key headings; greys only for hairline rules and disabled text. No gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners (radius 0), 1px black hairlines for separation.
- Type: monospaced, **IBM Plex Mono** (SIL OFL 1.1) self-hosted in `apps/web/public/fonts/` with its OFL notice; body 14px/21px regular; labels and links 12px UPPERCASE regular; buttons 14px uppercase medium; page headings uppercase, weight 500, tight tracking (about -0.05em) at 32 to 40px. System sans (Helvetica, Arial) as the fallback stack. No proprietary fonts (the reference uses a proprietary serif; do not copy it).
- Layout: thin full-width header (wordmark left, icon links right), generous white space, content in a strict grid, full-bleed image tiles with small white boxed text buttons (white fill, black text, 1px black border, uppercase) laid over them; tiles of items use the category illustrations tinted in the item's colour (R6b) or a flat tinted tile until then.
- Never copy: the reference's name, logo, trademark symbol, diagonal stripes, quotation-mark word styling, arrows, photographs or copy. Wordmark is "SPARTA" in the same mono. A visible "DEMO" label sits in the header while the build is Release 0.
- Accessibility is unchanged and enforced: contrast tests stay green (`apps/web/components/contrast.test.ts` may be extended, never loosened), focus rings visible (2px blue or black outline), touch targets 44px, reduced-motion respected, no text below 12px, mono text left-aligned (no justified text).
