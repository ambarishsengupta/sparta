## 1. Parties and purpose

This agreement is between the **Sparta project team** (three students in the Analytics Capstone Project, Term 5, 2026; "we") and the **vendor** named on the invitation ("you"). It sets the rules for your free participation in the Sparta pilot, an **academic proof of concept** that tests whether anonymised group data from consumers' digital wardrobes helps vendors plan design, inventory and stocking.

## 2. What we provide

During the pilot (about 3 months from your invitation) we give you:
- read-only access to the vendor dashboard: ownership mix, purchase and wishlist trends, gaps, and the condition spectrum;
- a gap and demand report, normally monthly;
- the ability to upload a product feed so your items appear in the consumer catalogue.

There is **no fee** during the pilot. We make no promise to continue, to offer paid plans, or to keep any feature.

## 3. The data you see

- You see **aggregate group figures only**. No figure relates to fewer than 10 participants, and numbers are rounded to the nearest 5.
- Participants have been told that group figures are shared with vendors and have agreed to take part.
- The figures come from a **small pilot group** (about 100 participants, mostly in the countries where the pilot is open, not representative of any market). They are **indicative, not statistically reliable** and not a substitute for your own analysis.

## 4. What you may do with it

You may:
- use the dashboard and reports internally to inform your planning;
- quote findings in general terms, if you state that they come from a small academic pilot of Sparta and do not present them as market data.

## 5. What you must not do

You must not:
- try to identify, contact or track any participant, or to work out any individual's data from the figures, for example by combining filters, repeating queries or linking with other data;
- try to access data other than through the dashboard and reports, or to get around the group-size or rounding rules;
- run automated queries or scrape the dashboard (usage is logged and rate limited);
- share your login, or give the figures to third parties for resale or publication as data;
- use the portal for any unlawful purpose.

If you suspect that a figure could reveal an individual, tell us at once and stop using it.

## 6. Your product feed

If you upload a product feed:
- you confirm you have the right to supply it and let us display product attributes (name, brand, category, colour, sizes, price, link) in the app;
- we show **attributes and a link to your product page**. We do not fetch or store your product images unless you separately agree;
- you are responsible for the accuracy of your feed, and you may update or withdraw it at any time. Withdrawn items are removed from the catalogue.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public information (for example unreleased products, or the content of unpublished reports) confidential and use it only for the pilot. This does not cover information that is public, already known, independently developed, or that the law requires to be disclosed.

## 8. Academic use

The team will write an academic report on the pilot. The report uses only aggregate figures and general observations. We will not name you or publish your feed without your written permission. We may ask you for feedback on whether the insights would change a decision, and quote it only with your permission.

## 9. Intellectual property

You keep your rights in your brand and feed content. We keep our rights in the Sparta software, which is open source under the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPL-3.0). Group figures and reports are provided to you for the use in section 4.

## 10. No warranty, limited liability

The pilot, figures and reports are provided **as is**, without warranties of any kind, including accuracy, fitness for a purpose or availability. To the fullest extent the law allows, the project team is not liable for any loss or decision arising from using the pilot. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

## 11. Data protection

We handle participants' data as described in the Sparta Privacy Policy. You will not receive any personal data. If you receive information that appears to identify a person, tell us, and delete it.

## 12. Term and ending

This agreement runs for the duration of the pilot, and either party may end it at any time by notice through the contact address in the portal. On ending, your access stops, and we withdraw your feed from the catalogue unless you ask us to keep it for the rest of the pilot. Sections 5, 7, 8, 10 and 13 continue after the agreement ends.
