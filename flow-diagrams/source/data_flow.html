Controls that apply along the whole flow - Licence: raw H&M files stay out of git; the rules allow non-commercial research and education only. Derived files are not published.
- Privacy: customer IDs are already hashed; no attempt to re-identify; postal code and free text are dropped; under-18 customers are removed; no personal data in the repository (DPDP Act, 2023 statement in the report).
- Provenance: SHA-256 recorded for every raw and cleaned file; the cleaning script rebuilds the cleaned dataset (same input, same checksums).
- Scope: H&M is one brand's mostly European customers, a stand-in for purchase demand and not for Sparta users. Fashion-MNIST holds no purchase data and says nothing about which clothes go together.
- customers.csv has no gender field, so menswear is defined by article (index group), not by customer; some buyers of menswear are not men.
- Dashed boxes are not part of the cleaning: they are offline or not yet available.
