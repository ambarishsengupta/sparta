Connections

    Rules that bind every agent

    • Maker and checker are never the same agent; a checker fails work only by citing a script result or a quoted requirement.
    • At most two fix loops per work order, then the orchestrator splits, re-plans or escalates to the owner.
    • No participant data in training, ever. H&M data stays in its folder, and the H&M-trained files stay off main and out of every pilot build, and the learned parts stay off in a pilot or production build, until the licence decision (G7) is made by people.
    • Stay in the project folder, no Claude attribution lines in commits, outward actions need a yes, investigations are time-boxed to 10 minutes.
    • Accounts and keys are a human job (owner and SA); agents never create an account or enter a key.
    • An Opus agent approves every release (G6) and the workflow approver, never the retro agent that proposed it, approves every change to this workflow (G8); the product owner has the last word. The style specialist reviews 50 suggestions in R3 and R4 (G9).
