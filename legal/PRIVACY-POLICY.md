## 1. In short

- Sparta is a **free pilot study** run by a student team as an academic proof of concept. It is not a commercial product.
- You keep a digital wardrobe: what you own, what you bought and where, what you want, and your outfits.
- To make the study work, **anonymised, grouped-together information** from all participants is shared with brands and retailers (the "vendors"). Vendors never see you, your photos, your receipts or your individual wardrobe.
- Taking part means accepting this sharing. You can **leave at any time** with the "Leave the pilot" button, which deletes your account and data.
- We do not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models in this pilot.

## 4. How we use it

1. **To run the app for you** (Terms of Use, section 5).
2. **To produce anonymised group insights for vendors.** Each night we combine data from all participants into groups such as "navy shirts, regular fit". A group is shown to vendors only if at least **10 participants** are in it, numbers are rounded to the nearest 5, and neighbouring groups can be hidden to make it hard to work out a hidden figure by subtraction. Vendors see only these group figures, never any row about you. Your user identity is replaced by a one-way code that changes on every run. Together these safeguards make it hard, though not impossible, for anyone to pick out one participant.
3. **To write our academic report.** The report, and any data we hand in for the course, use only aggregated figures: group figures built under the same rules as above, and overall pilot statistics such as how many people joined and how actively the app was used. They never include information about an individual participant. Faculty may see these figures.
4. **To keep the pilot secure and fix problems.**

We do not sell your data, show you advertising, or let vendors contact you. We do not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models. If a later phase wants to, we will ask you again with a new notice, and you can say no and leave.

**Why taking part means sharing.** The point of the pilot is to find out whether group data from real wardrobes is useful to vendors. Without it there is nothing to test, so sharing is part of taking part and cannot be switched off while you are in the pilot. The full product, if it ever exists, is planned to let each user turn sharing off.

We use these providers to run the pilot. They process data for us under their standard data processing terms.

| Provider | Role |

| Supabase | Database, sign-in and file storage. Hosted in South Asia (Mumbai, India). |

Your data may be processed outside your country, depending on the provider's region. We choose a region suited to the participants' country wherever possible.

**When the pilot ends** (planned after 3 months), we will tell you and you can export your data. We then either close the pilot and delete all participants' data within 30 days, or ask each participant whether to continue under a new notice, and delete the data of everyone who does not agree.

## 8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **See and correct** your data in the app.
- **Export** your data (Settings, Export my data).
- **Delete** your account and data (Settings, Leave the pilot). Deletion completes within 7 days; backups follow within 30 days.
- **Leave the pilot** with no penalty and no need to give a reason.
- **Ask a question or complain** at the contact address (section 2). If you are not satisfied, you may contact the data protection authority in your country.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under the law that applies to you.

## 9. Security

Your data is stored in access-controlled services, with rules in the database so that each participant can reach only their own rows. Traffic is encrypted. Photos and receipts are stored in private storage. We follow an incident plan: if your data is affected by a breach, we will tell you without undue delay and as the law requires. No system is perfectly secure, and this is a student pilot.

## 10. Who can take part

You must be **18 or over**, invited by the team, and living in a country where the pilot is open (the list is shown at sign-up). We rely on your declaration of age and country. If you are under 18, please do not use the app.

## 11. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in and to make the app work offline. To work offline, the app keeps a copy of your recently viewed wardrobe, outfits and wishlist, and any changes not yet synced, in your browser on that device. It is removed when you sign out or leave the pilot. If you share a device, sign out when you finish. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Usage events are our own and are pseudonymous.

## 12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will show a new notice and ask you to accept it again. If you do not accept, you can leave and delete your account.
