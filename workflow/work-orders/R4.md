Requested by: product owner delegation of 2026-11-04. Workflow v0.4; conventions in R0.md. Learned parts stay OFF by default; nothing H&M-derived is committed or pinned (G4, BR-59).

## Goal
Complete the look and Wardrobe unlock work in the engine and the app with every learned part switched off, and the pairing prior can be built from the H&M data, evaluated and switched on locally.

## Completion conditions
- CC1: `bash workflow/gates/gate.sh` exits 0.
- CC2: `python3 -m unittest discover -s ml/recommendation-engine/pairing/tests` passes (synthetic fixtures; at least 12 tests).
- CC3: `python3 ml/recommendation-engine/pairing/build_prior.py` (paths from workflow/local.env) writes `pairing-prior-v1.json` and `unlock-candidates.json` into `$SPARTA_ARTIFACT_DIR` (git-ignored) and writes `ml/recommendation-engine/pairing/PRIOR_CARD.md` (committed) with: overall AUC and a customer-bootstrap 95% interval, AUC per slot pair, basket and pair counts, the 20 highest and 20 lowest type pairs, the SHA-256 of articles.csv, customers.csv and transactions_train.csv, and the verdict `ships` when AUC >= 0.60 else `not shippable`. Two runs give byte-identical JSON.
- CC4: `python3 ml/recommendation-engine/pairing/validate_prior.py "$SPARTA_ARTIFACT_DIR/pairing-prior-v1.json"` exits 0 (schema, ranges 0..1, every key a Sparta sub-type or colour family from packages/taxonomy, no date or hash fields).
- CC5: `git ls-files | grep -E "pairing-prior-v|unlock-candidates"` prints nothing; the committed PRIOR_CARD.md contains no 10-digit article id and no customer id.
- CC6: `npm test -w @sparta/rules` passes with the new tests: with the prior off, `suggest` output is identical to R1/R3 (same ids, order, scores) on 200 random wardrobes; with a fixture prior on, `weights_with_pairing` is used and `pairing` appears in the score parts; the prior never admits an invalid outfit (property test); the prior file missing or invalid falls back to prior-off.
- CC7: Complete the look: every outfit returned for an anchor contains the anchor in its slot and passes R1, R2 and the slots; an anchor that fails R1 or R2 returns `anchor_not_valid`; anchors that are accessories, underwear or socks are not offered; Sweater, Cardigan and Hoodie are tried in both top and layer placements.
- CC8: Wardrobe unlock per LLD 9.5.2: at most one entry per sub-type, at most three results, "exactly N new outfits" equals an INDEPENDENT brute-force counter written in the test file (not importing the engine's counting code) on at least 50 random wardrobes; two runs give the same list; `unlock-fallback.json` (committed, at most 40 entries, taxonomy values only, includes Chinos, Joggers, Formal shoes, Sandals) is used when the switch is `fallback`; a unit test proves unlock works with all four switches off; `unlock` on a 500-item wardrobe finishes under 1 s.
- CC9: wishlist entries created from unlock carry `origin: 'unlock'` and the app's local wishlist aggregate excludes them (a test).
- CC10: the web app (built in R3) shows Complete the look and Wardrobe unlock; `npm run build -w apps/web` exits 0; the demo evidence in workflow/evidence/R4/ shows both features working with all switches off and, separately, with the locally built prior on.
