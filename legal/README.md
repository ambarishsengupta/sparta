# Legal and privacy documents (pilot drafts)

These documents support the Sparta pilot, an **academic proof of concept**. They were drafted by the project team with AI assistance, because the team has no legal expert. **They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by a lawyer.** The product owner has decided that a professional review is out of scope for the pilot and is required before any public, commercial or wider launch.

| Document | What it is | Used where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Privacy Policy and Participant Information](PRIVACY-POLICY.md) | What is collected, why, who sees it, how long it is kept, user rights; includes the first-login notice text | Linked from the app; the notice is the first-login pop-up (FR-3) |
| [Terms of Use](TERMS-OF-USE.md) | The rules for using the pilot app | Accepted at sign-up (FR-68) |
| [Vendor Pilot Agreement](VENDOR-PILOT-AGREEMENT.md) | The rules for vendors in the pilot | Accepted at first login to the vendor portal (FR-94) |
| [Incident Response Plan](INCIDENT-RESPONSE-PLAN.md) | What the team does when something goes wrong | Internal |
| [Privacy Risk Assessment](PRIVACY-RISK-ASSESSMENT.md) | A lightweight impact assessment, including re-identification risks | Internal; for the professional review |
| [Data Sourcing Policy](DATA-SOURCING-POLICY.md) | Rules for the seed catalogue, the collector and vendor feeds | Internal; drives the collector |
| [Legal and Privacy Review Log](LEGAL-REVIEW.md) | The review findings and what was done about each | Internal; for the professional review |

Design changes that follow from this review are in BRD v1.1, FDD v1.2, HLD v1.1 and LLD v1.1.
